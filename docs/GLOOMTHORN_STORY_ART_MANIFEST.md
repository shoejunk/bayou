## Production contract

The active runtime has 114 scenarios: 30 Mirewatch entries, 27 Blackthorn entries, and 57 Seelie entries. Every active scenario has dedicated custom art: 113 single-scene 1024x1536 masters plus the four-image River Teeth sequence, for 117 unique active masters. The Seelie table also preserves one commissioned master whose story beats were merged into a neighboring active scene; that retained file remains a versioned reference, not a runtime node. Each illustrated row is anchored to `src/client/client_story_catalog.cpp`, and the tactical/StoryOnly boundary is cross-checked against `docs/GLOOMTHORN_STORY_MODE_VALIDATION.md`.

- Except for the existing four-image River Teeth sequence, produce one 1024x1536 portrait cinematic-painterly-realism scenario illustration named `scenario_vN.png` and reuse the currently mapped version across that scenario's briefing, in-mission story, and aftermath panels. New revisions never overwrite prior masters.
- Shared StoryOnly IDs are separate runtime entries in Mirewatch and Blackthorn, so each campaign receives its own versioned output path even when the narrative beat is the same.
- Art may show story objects, evidence, injuries, custody, votes, machinery, or environmental danger. It must not imply that a player clicks those objects or that they are game rules.
- `READY` means the beat and principal designs are sufficiently anchored by current user-supplied art. `REFERENCE HOLD` means a named focal character has no current source-of-truth design. `GENERATED` means the new scenario asset exists and has been accepted; `EXISTING` identifies the approved four-panel River Teeth set. Every queued scenario is now `GENERATED` or `EXISTING`; no row remains on `READY` or `REFERENCE HOLD`.
